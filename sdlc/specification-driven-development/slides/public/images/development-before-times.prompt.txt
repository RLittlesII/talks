Use case: infographic-diagram
Asset type: wide editorial illustration strip for a technical conference Slidev presentation, four illustrated timeline panels with NO text; headings and captions are added separately in HTML.
Primary request: paint the evolution of a software developer's working life: writing programs on paper, punching cards, the monumental specification binder of the 1990s, then the satirical destruction and fragmentation of the specification in Agile-era practice.
Composition: extremely wide landscape, approximately 3:1, four equal-width scenes in one row, clearly separated by subtle vertical rules. Each scene centered in its own quarter; generous clear margins; no scene bleeds into the next. All important objects entirely visible. Same recurring friendly adult developer character ages and changes clothes with the era.
Panel 1, far left: mid-century programmer at a wooden desk carefully handwriting a program and simple flowchart on large paper coding sheets, pencil in hand, no computer or punch cards.
Panel 2, center-left: programmer operates a historically recognizable keypunch machine with a keyboard, rectangular punched cards with visible holes, and a tidy card deck; batch-era computing cabinet behind.
Panel 3, center-right: 1990s developer at a beige CRT computer reverently presents a comically enormous three-ring specification binder on a pedestal-like stack of documents; precise page tabs, organized diagrams and checkboxes, binder glows warmly like a sacred artifact. Readable visual of the wonder and burden of a huge specification.
Panel 4, far right: modern developer next to a paper shredder devouring that same giant binder's pages, loose strips fly toward a board of scattered colorful sticky-note tickets; developer's raised eyebrow and open hands convey 'where did the whole picture go?' Wry satire of documentation discarded in practice. Do not depict fire, violence or literal harm.
Style/medium: polished hand-drawn editorial cartoon with strong clean outlines, expressive people, restrained shading, subtle paper grain, intelligent conference infographic rather than corporate clip art.
Color palette: deep charcoal navy backdrop #161b26, warm parchment paper, muted teal, gold #e2b96f highlights, rust accents for shredded-document panel. High contrast and legible silhouettes at projector size.
Constraints: Four panels exactly, arranged in the requested order. No words, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no watermarks, no captions, no title, no arrows. Not a factual claim that Agile requires destroying documents; this is a deliberately satirical visual of an industry habit.
